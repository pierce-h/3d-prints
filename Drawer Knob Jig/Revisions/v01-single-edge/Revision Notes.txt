DRAWER KNOB JIG v01 — SINGLE TOP-EDGE STOP
November 18, 2026

New drawer-specific model derived from the successful cabinet jig's guide size.
The user confirmed a 63.5 mm (2 1/2-inch) top rail and a hole centered 31.75 mm
(1 1/4 inches) below its top edge. Drawer midpoint is measured independently.

One 80 mm-wide top-edge stop replaces the cabinet jig's two corner stops.
The jig slides freely sideways. A recessed vertical centerline and open V-notch
share the guide's X axis, so they can line up with a pencil midpoint mark.
Body: 80 x 59 x 20 mm, with a 10 mm-thick plate and 10 mm stop extension.
The plate reaches only 55 mm down the drawer, staying on the confirmed rail.
The nominal 5.05 mm guide and 0.5 mm entry chamfer match the tested cabinet jig.

Saved 3MF: clear Bambu PETG Translucent, H2D right nozzle, 0.20 mm layers,
6 walls, 25% gyroid, 5 top/bottom layers, 3 mm brim, no supports or prime tower.
Slice passed without warnings: 1 h 34 min 5 sec / 31.81 g.
STL is the same one-piece geometry in its print pose; 3MF retains the profile.
