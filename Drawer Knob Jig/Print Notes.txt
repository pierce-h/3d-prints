PLACEMENT AND USE
The user confirmed a 2 1/2-inch (63.5 mm) drawer top rail. The single drill
guide is 1 1/4 inches (31.75 mm) below the drawer's top edge, centered on that
rail. This is a different placement from the Claybourne cabinet-door jig.
One straight stop bears against the top edge; there is no left/right side stop.

1. Measure the drawer's width and mark its midpoint with a short vertical pencil
   line on the rail. The jig does not measure or find the drawer midpoint.
2. Seat the jig flat against the front with its stop fully against the top edge.
3. Slide it sideways until the V-notch apex and engraved centerline align with
   the pencil line. Clamp without shifting the jig; use a backer behind the wood.
4. Drill through the guide with the same 3/16-inch bit used with the cabinet jig.

DIMENSIONS
Overall: 80 mm wide x 59 mm tall x 20 mm total depth.
Front plate: 10 mm thick; reaches 55 mm below the top edge, within the rail.
Top stop: 4 mm thick and extends 10 mm behind the contact face.
Guide: nominal 5.05 mm bore, 0.5 mm entry chamfer, centered across the jig.
Alignment: 0.6 mm-wide engraved line and 3 mm-wide/deep open V at the lower edge.
The 5.05 mm guide and 10 mm plate thickness match the cabinet jig the user
reported printing and using successfully on November 18, 2026.

PRINT SETUP
Saved clear PETG profile is inherited from Cabinet Knob Jig v03: 0.20 mm layers,
6 walls, 25% gyroid, 5 top/bottom layers, 3 mm brim, 0.4 mm right nozzle.
Print the broad exposed drill face on the bed, stop pointing up.
Select the actual clear spool when sending; previous device inspection found
it in AMS A3, but send-time mapping must be checked. No print was sent for v01.

Check seating and bit clearance before drilling. The guide is printed plastic;
replace it if wear makes it loose. Recessed drawer panels do not support the
jig, so its 55 mm reach intentionally stays within the confirmed 63.5 mm rail.
