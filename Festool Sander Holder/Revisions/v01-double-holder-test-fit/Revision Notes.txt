DESIGN
One integrated print for Festool RO 150 ROTEX and ETS EC 150/5.
Two copies of the original cradle; tool-facing rim/cavity preserved.
Original RO150 engraving remains on both cradles.
Obsolete screw holes and countersinks are filled.
Overall installed dimensions: 348 x 81.984 x 43.5 mm.
Cradle spacing: 184 mm center-to-center; 20 mm between outside edges.
Added shared rear plate: 6 mm thick, joined across both cradles.
Rear cleat: 314 mm wide, 17.5 mm projection, 45-degree mating bevel.
Designed around user-reported nominal 3/4-inch plywood, 45-degree bevel.
Rear hook clears an ideal 19.05 mm wall rail by 1.55 mm, or an 18 mm rail by 0.5 mm.
Below the bevel, the rear plate braces against the FRONT face of the wooden
wall cleat. It does not require a full-depth spacer down to the wall itself.
Wall-cleat height was not supplied; the available bracing area depends on it.

PRINT PROJECTS
Saved machine: Bambu Lab H2D, 0.4 mm nozzle.
Assigned material: Bambu ABS from the user's original project.
0.20 mm layers, 6 walls, 40% gyroid infill, 6 top and bottom shell layers.
The full holder is top-edge-down and rotated 43 degrees to fit the plate.
Automatic normal support is enabled for the original curved cradle overhangs.
The cleat itself starts on the bed and its bevel recedes during printing.
5 mm outer brim. Small sample uses the same mating profile without supports.
Do not rotate the full holder flat onto its rear without reviewing supports.

SLICER ESTIMATES (saved profile; actual time/use may vary)
Full holder: approximately 19h 57m, 353 g ABS including support/brim.
Cleat sample: approximately 2h 18m, 30 g ABS.
Both CLI slices completed successfully with empty warning_message fields.
The full holder was also opened and sliced in desktop Bambu Studio; its
0.20 mm layer height and 19h57m / 353.36 g estimate were confirmed on screen.
Model, support and brim extrusion paths fit the common area of both nozzles.
The CLI logs emitted Invalid T command messages for H2D-specific commands in
the supplied machine profile, while both result.json files report Success.
No G-code was sent to a printer. Slice again in Bambu Studio for your actual setup.
Bambu Studio displays an ABS/high-temperature-filament advisory recommending
nozzle-clumping detection via probing. This is a printer/profile choice to
review before printing; the model edit did not enable or disable that feature.
The desktop app also reports automatically disabling arc fitting for the
connected printer's curve-planning feature.

PHYSICAL CHECKS BEFORE THE FULL PRINT
1. Print the small sample. It should slide down onto the rail, seat on the
   bevel, and sit with its lower back flat against the rail's front face.
   Check that its rear tip clears the wall and that it does not rock.
2. Using the existing holders/tools, check both tools at 184 mm pad-center
   spacing. Verify clearance for handles, housings, hoses and removal.
3. If either check fails, record the measured dimensions so a new revision
   can be made. The 20 mm gap is a design assumption, not a measured tool clearance.
4. After printing the full holder, remove rim supports and check both tools'
   seating and the mounted holder under a gradual, attended load before normal use.
   This prototype has no verified load rating or long-term creep test.
