HOW THIS LIBRARY IS ORGANIZED
Each model has its own folder:
  Original/     Unmodified starting files.
  Source/       Editable CAD files or modeling scripts and their dependencies.
  Revisions/    Numbered versions with Bambu projects, STL exports, previews,
                and validation records.
  Print Notes.txt  Revision status and actual print/fit feedback.

Use a new numbered revision for meaningful geometry or saved print-setting changes.
Keep earlier revisions and original files. Update this index when the latest
revision changes. Mark a revision TESTED SUCCESSFULLY only after print/fit feedback.
Keep the entire library in your normal backup routine.
