DESIGN
Two mirrored single-hole jigs share one print plate. Looking at the exposed
drill face, with the stops behind the plate against the door edges:
A / one groove fits top-left and bottom-right; B / two grooves fits top-right
and bottom-left. Rotate 180 degrees for the diagonally opposite corner.
Guide center: 31.75 mm from side, 63.5 mm from end. The 10 mm-thick plate
extends 70 x 90 mm inward from the edge datums; overall size is 74 x 94 x 20 mm. Edge stops extend 10 mm behind the contact face,
accommodating the confirmed 19.05–20.6375 mm doors without a fitted channel.

A 5.05 mm nominal bore with a small entry chamfer guides a 3/16-inch (4.7625 mm)
bit. This is a printed guide, not a metal bushing. Check free bit movement with
the motor off, then test positioning and actual screw clearance on scrap before
finished doors. Clamp the jig and back the exit face with scrap. Use light
pressure, clear chips and replace a worn guide. No screw thread type is assumed.

PRINT AND VALIDATION
H2D / 0.4 mm / PLA Basic / LEFT nozzle, Manual grouping. 0.20 mm layers,
6 walls, 25% gyroid, 5 top/bottom layers, 3 mm brim, supports disabled.
Print broad exposed faces on the bed, with stops rising above the plates.
The final slice succeeded with no warnings or support toolpaths, using only
PLA on the left nozzle. Independent mesh review is recorded in Validation.
