POSITION AND HARDWARE
Side offset: 31.75 mm (1 1/4 in). End offset: 71.501 mm (2.815 in,
approximately 2 13/16 in) = nominal 63.5 mm rail + 8.001 mm base radius.
Claybourne published base: 0.63 in (16.002 mm); face: 1.25 in (31.75 mm);
projection: 1 in (25.4 mm). The larger face overlaps the rail reference line by
7.874 mm. This revision aligns the mounting base, not the face edge.
Actual stile/rail widths remain approximate; check the intended location before
finished doors. Confirmed door thickness is 19.05–20.6375 mm.

A / one groove fits top-left and bottom-right; B / two grooves fits top-right
and bottom-left, viewed from the exposed drill face. Rotate 180 degrees for
the opposite corner. Each jig has one guide. Overall dimensions: 74 x 94 x 20 mm.
The 10 mm-thick plate reaches 70 x 90 mm inward; stops project 10 mm behind it.

The manufacturer specifies #8-32 screws and a 3/16-inch bit (4.7625 mm).
Printed guide bore: 5.05 mm, with 0.5 mm entry chamfer. Test free bit movement
with the motor off and check position/screw clearance on scrap. Clamp the jig
and back the exit face. Printed guides can wear; replace if loose or damaged.
