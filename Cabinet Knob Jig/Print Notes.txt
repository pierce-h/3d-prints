HOLE POSITION
The guide center is 31.75 mm (1 1/4 in) from the side edge and 63.5 mm (2 1/2 in)
from the top/bottom edge. This is the recommended layout chosen after the user
asked for standard placement. It centers on a nominal 2 1/2-inch stile.
Use the upper corner of base-cabinet doors and the lower corner of wall-cabinet
doors, opposite the hinges. Verify the appearance and actual stile center on
one door before using the fixed offsets throughout the project.

JIG IDENTIFICATION — VIEWED FROM THE EXPOSED DRILL FACE
A, one engraved groove: top-left or bottom-right corner.
B, two engraved grooves: top-right or bottom-left corner.
Rotate the appropriate jig 180 degrees for its diagonally opposite corner.
Each jig has one drill guide.

DOOR FIT AND DIMENSIONS
Confirmed door thickness: 12/16–13/16 in (19.05–20.6375 mm).
Reported stile and rail width: roughly 2 1/2 in (63.5 mm).
The plate extends 70 x 90 mm inward from the edge datums and is 10 mm thick.
Including the fences, the overall envelope is 74 x 94 x 20 mm.
Two edge stops extend 10 mm behind the door-contact face. They register against
the outer edges, without fitting around the door's full thickness. A small
corner relief helps both stops seat. Flat outer-edge registration is required.

DRILL GUIDE
Use a 3/16-inch bit (4.7625 mm). The modeled guide bore is 5.05 mm with a small
entry chamfer, through the 10 mm plate. That CAD allowance is provisional;
printed holes are not guaranteed to match it exactly.
The screw measures roughly 1/8 in; its thread type is unconfirmed. Check on
scrap that it passes freely through the drilled hole and the hardware covers it.

This version has a printed-plastic guide, not a metal drill bushing. With the
motor off, the bit must slide through by hand without binding. Clamp the jig
securely over the solid stile/rail and support the door's exit face with scrap.
Drill with light pressure and clear chips. Inspect the guide for wear; replace
it if loose or damaged.

PRINT SETUP AND CHECKS
Bambu H2D, standard 0.4 mm nozzle, PLA Basic on the LEFT nozzle, filament 1,
Manual grouping. 0.20 mm layers, 6 walls, 25% gyroid, 5 top/bottom layers,
3 mm outer brim; supports disabled. Both broad exposed faces lie on the bed,
with the edge stops rising above the plates. Preserve that pose.
The final slice succeeded with no warnings or support toolpaths, using only
PLA on the left nozzle. Independent mesh review is recorded in Validation.
Check the actual filament and printer mapping before sending a print.
