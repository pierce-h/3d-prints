CLAYBOURNE PLACEMENT
Guide center: 31.75 mm (1 1/4 in) from the side and 71.501 mm (2.815 in,
approximately 2 13/16 in) from the top/bottom edge.
Rejuvenation's Claybourne specification gives a 0.63-inch mounting base
(16.002 mm), a 1.25-inch knob face (31.75 mm), and 1-inch projection (25.4 mm).
The end offset is the nominal 63.5 mm rail width plus the 8.001 mm base radius.
This places the mounting-base edge on the rail's inner edge. The larger knob
face deliberately overlaps that line by 7.874 mm; its edge is not the datum.
The hole moves 8.001 mm farther from the end than v01. Side offset is unchanged.

Use the upper corner of base-cabinet doors and the lower corner of wall-cabinet
doors, opposite the hinges. Stile and rail widths remain roughly 2 1/2 inches;
verify the actual centerline and base alignment on one door before drilling.

JIG IDENTIFICATION — VIEWED FROM THE EXPOSED DRILL FACE
A, one engraved groove: top-left or bottom-right corner.
B, two engraved grooves: top-right or bottom-left corner.
Rotate 180 degrees for the diagonally opposite corner. Each jig has one guide.

DOOR FIT
Confirmed door thickness: 12/16–13/16 in (19.05–20.6375 mm).
The plate extends 70 x 90 mm inward from the edge datums and is 10 mm thick;
overall envelope including fences is 74 x 94 x 20 mm. Edge stops extend 10 mm
behind the contact face. Flat outer edges register the jig without a fitted
full-thickness channel. Corner relief helps both stops seat.

DRILL GUIDE AND USE
Claybourne's installation instructions specify #8-32 screws and a 3/16-inch
bit (4.7625 mm). The printed guide is nominally 5.05 mm through the 10 mm plate,
with a 0.5 mm entry chamfer. Check free bit movement with the motor off, then
check hole position, screw clearance and base alignment on scrap.
The guide is printed plastic, not a metal bushing. Clamp the jig over the solid
stile/rail and back the door's exit face with scrap. Use light pressure, clear
chips and replace a worn or loose guide.
