CHANGE
The original physical print was too tight for the user's caliper body. Its
center opening measured 28.92079 mm in the model. The revised opening is 32 mm,
providing approximately 1 mm clearance per side for a 30 mm body.
Only the inner front support tips were trimmed, about 1.5396 mm per side.
Backplate, French cleat, outside dimensions and original print placement remain
unchanged. Overall mounted size: 80 mm wide x 95 mm high x 32 mm deep.

SAVED PRINT SETTINGS
Original Bambu H2D 0.4 mm profile; black Bambu PLA Basic, project filament slot 1.
0.20 mm layers, 4 walls, 20% gyroid; automatic tree supports and automatic brim.
The original printer, material and process settings were retained.

FIT CHECK AFTER PRINTING
Remove support remnants and test the caliper body between the front supports.
It should drop into position without forcing and remain supported by the holder.
Confirm easy removal and proper seating on the existing French cleat.
The revised fit has not been physically tested. No print was sent to a printer.
