The user's red-rack reference clarified that tools should rest upright in a
closed-bottom tray with tall dividers. v02 replaces the v01 hanging-comb
concept. This is original CAD inspired by the photo, not an exact measured
product copy or a downloaded third-party mesh. v01 remains archived unchanged.

DESIGN
Each tray: 266 mm wide x 130 mm tall x 122.5 mm deep including French cleats;
floor footprint depth 105 mm. A 6 mm gap between trays gives 538 mm total width
(21.18 inches). Bay widths: 24/24/28/28/32/32/36/36 mm. Actual tool widths are
unknown; capacities are provisional. Tools rest on the solid floor, choosing
whichever end/orientation seats stably. There is no head-width catch requirement.
Tall rear fins taper down toward an 18 mm front lip, 14.8 mm above the floor.
Floor/back: 3.2 mm. Internal fins: 2.8 mm. Outer walls: 3.2 mm.
Two 60 mm cleat segments per tray project 17.5 mm with 45-degree bevels, for
nominal 19.05 mm plywood. Local attachment pads are 6 mm thick with 45-degree
lower ramps. Actual rail dimensions and mating fit remain unconfirmed.

The 102 x 105 x 35 mm fit tray has 24/30/36 mm bays and no cleat. It checks
width and seating by hand/on a bench. It cannot test full-height lateral
stability, wall mounting or load capacity.

PRINT AND VALIDATION
Black Bambu PLA Basic / H2D standard 0.4 mm / LEFT extruder 1, project filament 1,
Manual grouping. 0.20 mm layers, 4 walls, 25% gyroid, 5 top/bottom layers, 4 mm
outer brim. The floor sits on the bed; automatic tree supports serve rear cleats.
All three final slices succeeded with no plate warnings using only PLA on the
left extruder. Full and single trays generate supports; the shallow sample does
not. The final G-code audit confirms all supports stay behind the trays,
with none inside the tool bays, and print paths fit the left-nozzle area.
Supports account for about 18 g of the full set (2% of its material).
See Validation/support_review.json in the v02 revision for the audit.
Geometry and independent review confirm closed floors and connected watertight
pieces. The full project intentionally has two separate trays. Per-project
slice evidence is stored under Validation/full, section and sample.
Four previews render actual CAD. Installed STLs in Validation are inspection
files; use the main 3MFs to retain print pose and settings.
