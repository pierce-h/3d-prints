PLIERS HOLDER — v01 SIXTEEN SLOTS
October 16, 2026

Original model for mixed-size pliers, including larger tools. No downloaded
third-party mesh is used. Actual tool dimensions were not provided.
Status: PROTOTYPE; geometry and slicing verified, physical fit/load untested.

DESIGN
Each section: 286 mm wide x 58 mm tall x 108.5 mm deep including cleats.
Two sections mounted 6 mm apart span 578 mm (22.76 inches). They mount separately.
Each has slots 12/14/16/18/20/22/24/26 mm wide, on 34 mm centers, maximum depth
69 mm. Shelf thickness is 6 mm, with 5 mm ribs under all nine shelf fingers.
The back is 4 mm thick, reinforced to 6 mm across the cleat height. Two 60 mm
rear cleat segments per section use a 17.5 mm projection and 45-degree bevel,
for nominal 19.05 mm plywood wall cleats. Actual cleat fit remains untested.
No fixed lower rear spacer is included because the rail height is unknown.

Jaws point up, handles down; broad flat faces face neighboring tools and handles
spread front-to-back. The tool head/joint must be wider than its chosen slot.
Slide tools fully back. There is no front retaining lip. These slot widths are
capacities, not measured fits; bulky pliers may need an empty adjacent slot.
The 116 x 91 x 10 mm handheld gauge checks three widths only. It has no French
cleat and does not test mounting or load capacity.

PRINT SETUP
Bambu H2D, standard 0.4 mm, black Bambu PLA Basic, LEFT nozzle / extruder 1.
Project filament 1, Manual filament grouping; confirm actual spool mapping.
0.20 mm layers, 4 walls, 25% gyroid, 5 top/bottom layers, 3 mm outer brim.
Shelf/top faces the bed. Supports are disabled and none appear in checked G-code.
