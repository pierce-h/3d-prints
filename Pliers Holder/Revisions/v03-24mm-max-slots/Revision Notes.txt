DIMENSIONS AND USE
Each tray's clear widths: 16/16/18/18/20/20/22/24 mm. Each tray is 180 mm wide,
177.8 mm (7 inches) tall and 63.5 mm (2.5 inches) deep, or 81 mm including cleats.
Two trays with a 6 mm gap span 366 mm (14.41 inches). The supplied reference
width was 304.8 mm; this 16-bay pair is wider because its overall width follows
the chosen bay widths plus walls. The reference divider thickness is unknown.
Trays mount independently.
Tools rest on a solid floor; choose an end/orientation that seats stably. Tall
rear dividers taper toward the front. Check leaning, removal and adjacent-tool
clearance using actual tools. No head-width catch is required.

Floor/back remain 3.2 mm thick, inner dividers 2.8 mm, outer walls 3.2 mm and
front lip 18 mm overall (14.8 mm above the floor). Each tray retains two 60 mm
French cleat segments with 17.5 mm projection and a 45-degree bevel for nominal
19.05 mm plywood. Local pads remain 6 mm thick with 45-degree lower ramps.
The cleats and pads move up 47.8 mm to match the height increase.

The 72 x 63.5 x 35 mm fit tray has 16/20/24 mm bays and no cleat. Use it by hand
or on a bench to check width and seating. Its shallow walls cannot test the
lateral stability of tall tools, full-height storage, wall mounting or loads.

PRINT AND CHECKS
Bambu H2D, standard 0.4 mm, black Bambu PLA Basic on LEFT extruder 1; project
filament 1, Manual grouping. 0.20 mm layers, 4 walls, 25% gyroid, 5 top/bottom
layers, 4 mm outer brim. Preserve the upright floor-down pose. Automatic tree
supports are enabled for rear cleats. Confirm the actual PLA spool/mapping.
All three final slices succeeded with no plate warnings using only PLA on the
left extruder. Full and single trays generate supports; the shallow sample does
not. Independent geometry review passed. The G-code audit confirms supports
stay behind the trays, outside all bays, and all printed features fit the
left-nozzle area. Full-set supports total 34.5 g; see support_review.json
in the revision Validation folder.
