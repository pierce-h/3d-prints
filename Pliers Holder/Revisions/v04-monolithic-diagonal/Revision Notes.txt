DIMENSIONS
356.4 mm (14.03 in) wide x 177.8 mm (7 in) tall; tray depth 63.5 mm (2.5 in),

16/16/16/16/18/18/18/18/20/20/20/20/22/22/24/24 mm.
Floor/back: 3.2 mm. Internal dividers: 2.8 mm. Outer walls: 3.2 mm.
Front lip: 18 mm overall, 14.8 mm above the floor. Tools rest on the floor;
choose an end/orientation that seats stably and check removal/leaning.

Three 60 mm French cleat segments, centers 45/178.2/311.4 mm from the left,
project 17.5 mm with 45-degree bevels for nominal 19.05 mm plywood. Local
attachment pads are 6 mm thick with lower 45-degree ramps. Cleat fit is untested.

The existing ../v03-24mm-max-slots/Pliers_Tray_v03_3_Bay_Fit_Sample_PLA_Left.3mf
remains the fit sample: 16/20/24 mm openings, 63.5 mm depth, 35 mm height, no
cleat. Its slice is 1 h 39 min 10 sec / 54.41 g. This shallow gauge checks width
and seating only, not tall-tool stability, wall mounting or full-holder loads.

PRINT SETUP
Bambu H2D / standard 0.4 mm / black Bambu PLA Basic / LEFT extruder 1, project
filament 1, Manual grouping. 0.20 mm layers, 4 walls, 25% gyroid, 5 top/bottom
layers and 4 mm outer brim. Floor-down with saved diagonal angle 45 degrees.
Tree supports are enabled for rear cleats. Preserve the checked placement.
The model is one connected watertight body. The final slice has no plate
warnings and uses only PLA on the left extruder. The G-code audit confirms
all supports stay behind the tray, outside its bays; support material is
21.70 g. All extrusion, including line widths, brim and supports, fits the
left-nozzle area. Minimum bed-edge margin is 1.89 mm, so preserve the saved
placement and brim. See Validation/support_review.json.
