This 72 mm-wide x 63.5 mm-deep x 35 mm-tall sample has 16/20/24 mm bays and no
cleat. It checks width and seating by hand or on a bench. Its shallow dividers
do not test lateral stability of tall tools, full-height storage or mounting.

SIZE AND USE
Each tray is 180 mm wide x 177.8 mm (7 inches) tall x 81 mm deep including cleats.
The tray/floor depth is 63.5 mm (2.5 inches). Two separately mounted trays with
a 6 mm gap span 366 mm (14.41 inches); they do not connect mechanically.
Each tray's clear bay widths are 16/16/18/18/20/20/22/24 mm, capped at the user's
requested 24 mm maximum. Actual tool fit remains untested.
The reference dimensions, 304.8 x 177.8 x 63.5 mm, establish proportions. Our
16-bay pair is wider: its overall width follows the chosen bay widths plus walls.
The reference divider thickness is unknown.

Tools rest on the solid floor between tall divider fins. Choose the end and
orientation that seat each tool stably; the head does not need to catch on a
narrow slot. Fins are tallest at the back and slope down toward the front.
Check fit, removal and leaning/tipping with actual tools. The front retaining
lip is 18 mm high overall, or 14.8 mm above the floor.

STRUCTURE AND CLEAT
Floor and back are 3.2 mm thick; internal fins are 2.8 mm and outer walls 3.2 mm.
Each tray has two 60 mm-wide French cleat segments projecting 17.5 mm, with a
45-degree mating profile for nominal 3/4-inch (19.05 mm) plywood. Local 6 mm
reinforcement pads cover the attachments, with 45-degree lower ramps. Cleats
and pads sit 47.8 mm higher than v02 to follow the taller tray.
Actual rail height, cleat seating and loaded behavior remain untested. These
features and digital checks do not establish a load rating.

PRINT SETUP AND CHECKS
Bambu H2D, standard 0.4 mm, black Bambu PLA Basic on the LEFT nozzle, project
filament 1 / extruder 1, Manual grouping. 0.20 mm layers, 4 walls, 25% gyroid,
5 top/bottom layers and 4 mm outer brim. Preserve the upright, floor-down pose.
Automatic tree supports are enabled for the full trays' rear cleats.
All three final slices succeeded with no plate warnings using only PLA on the
left extruder. Full and single trays generate supports; the shallow sample does
not. Independent geometry review passed. The G-code audit confirms supports
stay behind the trays, outside all bays, and all printed features fit the
left-nozzle area. Full-set supports total 34.5 g; see support_review.json
in the revision Validation folder.
Confirm the actual PLA spool and mapping before a print.

The individual tray and sample meshes are single watertight connected solids;
the full plate intentionally contains two separate trays. Validation contains
