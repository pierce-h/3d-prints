This 72 x 63.5 x 35 mm shallow tray has 16/20/24 mm bays and no cleat. It checks

but cannot test tall-tool stability, wall mounting or the full holder's load.

SIZE AND STORAGE
One holder: 356.4 mm (14.03 inches) wide x 177.8 mm (7 inches) tall.
Tray/floor depth: 63.5 mm (2.5 inches), or 81 mm including the cleats.
Sixteen clear slots from left to right: four 16 mm, four 18 mm, four 20 mm,
two 22 mm and two 24 mm. The largest opening is 24 mm as requested.
The reference dimensions establish height/depth proportions; overall width
follows the chosen bays plus walls. Actual tool fit remains untested.

Tools rest on the closed floor between dividers. Choose the end/orientation
that seats each tool stably; no head-width catch is required. The fins are
tallest at the back and slope toward the front. Check leaning, removal and
adjacent-tool clearance. The front lip is 18 mm high, 14.8 mm above the floor.

STRUCTURE AND CLEATS
Floor/back are 3.2 mm thick; internal dividers 2.8 mm and outer walls 3.2 mm.
Three 60 mm-wide French cleat segments project 17.5 mm, with a 45-degree mating
profile for nominal 3/4-inch (19.05 mm) plywood. Segment centers are 45, 178.2
and 311.4 mm from the left edge. Local 6 mm attachment pads have 45-degree lower
ramps. Actual rail height, cleat seating and loaded behavior remain untested.

PRINT SETUP AND CHECKS
Bambu H2D, standard 0.4 mm, black Bambu PLA Basic on the LEFT nozzle, project
filament 1 / extruder 1, Manual grouping. 0.20 mm layers, 4 walls, 25% gyroid,
5 top/bottom layers and 4 mm outer brim. The floor sits on the bed.
Saved diagonal angle: 45 degrees.
Automatic tree supports are enabled for rear cleats. Confirm the actual PLA
spool and nozzle mapping before printing.
The model is one connected watertight body. The final slice has no plate
warnings and uses only PLA on the left extruder. The G-code audit confirms
all supports stay behind the tray, outside its bays; support material is
21.70 g. All extrusion, including line widths, brim and supports, fits the
left-nozzle area. Minimum bed-edge margin is 1.89 mm, so preserve the saved
placement and brim. See Validation/support_review.json.
Main STL/3MF files use the print pose; Validation/Tray_Installed.stl is for
inspection. Mounted_Preview.png and Print_Preview.png under the v04 revision
show actual CAD. Their display colors are for visibility.
