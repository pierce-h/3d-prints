SIZE AND STORAGE
Each section is 286 mm wide x 58 mm tall x 108.5 mm deep, including the cleat.
A suggested 6 mm gap between the two separately mounted sections gives a total
width of 578 mm (22.76 inches). They do not join together mechanically.
Each section has eight slots, left to right: 12, 14, 16, 18, 20, 22, 24 and 26 mm.
Slot centers are 34 mm apart. Maximum opening depth is 69 mm, with rounded
closed ends and flared front entries. These widths are design capacities,
not measured fits for the user's tools. Large heads may require an empty
neighboring slot; 16 slots does not guarantee clearance for 16 bulky pliers.

Store tools with jaws up and handles down. Turn their broad flat faces toward
neighboring tools, with the handles spreading front-to-back. The head or joint
must be wider than the chosen slot to rest on the shelf. Slide tools fully
rearward. The fronts are open and there is no retaining lip.
Hold the fit gauge securely while checking a small, medium and large tool.
Check that each tool slides in, rests securely and can be removed without
binding. Also check head clearance beside neighboring tools on a full section.

STRUCTURE AND FRENCH CLEAT
The shelf is 6 mm thick, with a 5 mm-wide rib under each of its nine fingers.
The 4 mm back is reinforced to 6 mm across the full cleat height. Each section
has two 60 mm-wide rear cleat segments projecting 17.5 mm, using the existing
45-degree mating profile for the user's nominal 3/4-inch (19.05 mm) plywood rail.
Actual rail height and fit have not been measured. There is no fixed lower
rear spacer, since an unknown rail height could make one interfere. Check that
the bevel seats and the back rests against the rail without rocking before use.
These features are not a tested load rating.

PRINT SETUP AND VALIDATION
Bambu H2D, standard 0.4 mm nozzle, black Bambu PLA Basic on the LEFT nozzle,
project filament 1 / extruder 1. Manual filament grouping retains this mapping.
0.20 mm layers, 4 walls, 25% gyroid, 5 top/bottom layers, 3 mm outer brim.
The saved pose places the shelf top on the bed; supports are disabled. All
three checked slices succeeded with no plate warnings and no support toolpaths.
Confirm the actual PLA spool and mapping before sending a job.

Each section and the gauge is a single watertight connected solid. The full
plate intentionally contains two independent sections. Geometry, independent
review and per-project slice records are under Revisions/v01-16-slot/Validation/.
STLs contain geometry only; use the 3MFs to retain the saved setup. Installed
STLs under Validation are inspection files, not the saved print pose.
