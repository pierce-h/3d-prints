DESIGN AND FIT BASIS
Each holder is 71 mm wide x 51.18 mm high x 51 mm deep. The rounded backing is
6.5 mm thick and has two screw holes above the receiver. Holes are 53 mm apart,
4.8 mm through, with 9.5 mm diameter, 90-degree countersinks. Choose flat-head
screws that fit those holes; their length must suit the actual cabinet panel.

The receiver is preserved from jverlenich's published IP1251 attachment
holder. Its keyed bore is nominally 33.6 mm diameter, with 3.35 mm keyways and
a 39.2 mm overall keyed span. These are measurements of the source mesh,
not Shark manufacturing dimensions. The original creator printed in PLA;
an independent make reports successful accessory fit.

Shark lists the same Duster Crevice Tool 576CH1000 and Pet Multi-Tool
579CH1000 for IP1251 and IP3251. That supports trying this receiver with the
user's IP3251, but the actual accessories have not been test-fitted.
https://www.sharkninja.com/duster-crevice-tool/576CH1000.html
https://www.sharkninja.com/pet-multi-tool/579CH1000.html

PRINT SETUP
Saved for Bambu H2D, black PLA / LEFT nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, 5 walls,
25% gyroid, 5 top and bottom layers, 3 mm outer brim, supports disabled.
Both the backing and receiver start on the bed; the socket opening faces up.
A small inherited latch lip projects about 2.1 mm, so the model is not
entirely free of overhangs. Check its finish on the first holder.
Confirm actual filament mapping when preparing a future print.
Both projects slice successfully with no plate warnings or support toolpaths.
Single holder: 1 h 39 min 27 sec / 43.39 g.
Pair: 3 h 13 min 57 sec / 86.76 g.
Slicing records are under the revision's Validation/single and Validation/pair.
These are slicer estimates, not actual print results.

FIT AND INSTALLATION
Try both accessories in the single holder before committing to cabinet-hole
locations. Check that they seat, release, and clear the backing comfortably.
Position the holders with room for each tool's full body and upward removal,
then secure the empty holders using the two screw holes. Exact spacing depends
on the accessories and available cabinet space; the two holders are separate.
