DESIGN AND FIT BASIS
Overall: 171 mm wide x 51.18 mm high x 51 mm deep. The rounded backing is
6.5 mm thick and connects both receivers into one printed body. Two screw
holes above the receivers are 153 mm apart, 4.8 mm through, with 9.5 mm
diameter, 90-degree countersinks. Choose flat-head screws that fit those
holes; their length must suit the actual cabinet panel.

Socket centers are 100 mm apart, matching the v01 preview arrangement.
This spacing is a design assumption: the complete accessory bodies have
not been measured or tested side by side. Confirm both tools can seat and
lift out comfortably before drilling cabinet mounting holes.

Each receiver is preserved from jverlenich's published IP1251 attachment
holder. Its keyed bore is nominally 33.6 mm diameter, with 3.35 mm keyways and
a 39.2 mm overall keyed span. These are measurements of the source mesh,
not Shark manufacturing dimensions. The original creator printed in PLA;
an independent make reports successful accessory fit.

Shark lists the same Duster Crevice Tool 576CH1000 and Pet Multi-Tool
579CH1000 for IP1251 and IP3251. That supports trying this receiver with the
user's IP3251, but the actual accessories have not been test-fitted.
https://www.sharkninja.com/duster-crevice-tool/576CH1000.html
https://www.sharkninja.com/pet-multi-tool/579CH1000.html

PRINT SETUP
Saved for Bambu H2D, clear Bambu PETG Translucent / RIGHT nozzle,
0.20 mm layers, 5 walls,
25% gyroid, 5 top and bottom layers, 3 mm outer brim, supports disabled.
Both receivers and the shared backing start on the bed; socket openings
face up. Each inherited latch lip projects about 2.1 mm. Check its finish
on the first print. Uses the same material preset as the drawer jig:
250 C initial / 245 C thereafter, textured bed 70 C, maximum volumetric
speed 6 mm3/s. Confirm actual filament mapping before sending a job;
clear PETG was previously used in AMS A3, not checked live for this revision.
One object slices successfully without plate warnings or support toolpaths.
Estimate: 4 h 25 min 39 sec / 91.55 g. This is not an actual print result.
Slicing records are under Revisions/v03-clear-petg-right/Validation/.
The preview and STL in v02 remain the geometry reference; preview shading
is illustrative and does not predict clear PETG's appearance.

INSTALLATION
Test both accessories together in the finished mount before choosing cabinet-hole locations.
Leave room for the full tools and upward removal, then secure the empty
mount using its two countersunk screw holes. Mount with socket openings up.
