v02 — ONE CONTINUOUS CABINET MOUNT WITH TWO SOCKETS
Created: November 18, 2026

The user requested one piece. This revision replaces the two separate v01
backplates with one continuous rounded backing, while preserving the two
receiver shapes and the 100 mm center spacing shown in the earlier preview.
Only two outer mounting screw holes are needed in the shared backplate.

check the v02 mount's fixed spacing or simultaneous accessory clearance.

GEOMETRY
Overall: 171 mm wide x 51.18 mm high x 51 mm deep.
Backing: 6.5 mm thick. Socket centers: 100 mm apart.
Two screw holes: 153 mm center spacing, 4.8 mm bore, 9.5 mm countersinks at
90 degrees. Both screw holes sit above the receivers.
Original keyed socket and latch features are preserved. Source bore is
nominally 33.6 mm diameter; this is a mesh measurement, not a Shark drawing.

PRINT PROFILE
H2D, black PLA, LEFT nozzle; 0.20 mm layers, 5 walls, 25% gyroid, 5 top/bottom
layers. Upright sockets, 3 mm outer brim, supports disabled. Inherited latch
lips project roughly 2.1 mm and should be inspected on the first print.
Actual filament mapping must be checked before sending a job.

STATUS AND FIT LIMITS
Geometry and slicing checked. One object slices without plate warnings or
support toolpaths, using only Bambu PLA Basic / LEFT extruder 1.
Estimate: 3 h 23 min 31 sec / 95.19 g. This is not an actual print result.
No print sent. The user's socket fit, accessory release and installed use
remain untested. IP1251-to-IP3251 compatibility is inferred from identical
Shark accessory part numbers: Duster Crevice Tool 576CH1000 and Pet Multi-Tool
579CH1000. The 100 mm socket spacing comes from the prior preview arrangement;
clearance between the complete tool bodies has not been measured or verified.
Test both accessories together before drilling cabinet mounting holes.
