v01 — TWO INDEPENDENT CABINET SOCKETS
Created: November 18, 2026

Two identical compact screw-mounted holders for the Shark IP3251 accessories.
The user originally described male nubs; the design uses drop-in receiving
sockets because a published measured socket with reported fit is available.

FILES
Shark_Accessory_Mount_v01_Single_PLA_Left_TEST_FIT.3mf
  One complete holder; use it to check both accessories first.
Shark_Accessory_Mount_v01_Pair_PLA_Left_TEST_FIT.3mf
  Two separate holders arranged together on an H2D plate.
Matching STL exports contain the same geometry in print orientation.
Preview.png and Single_Preview.png show the actual mesh. Validation/ keeps inspection-coordinate
meshes, geometry checks, slicing evidence and archive hashes.

GEOMETRY
Each holder: 71 mm wide x 51.18 mm high x 51 mm deep.
Backing: 6.5 mm thick. Two screw holes: 53 mm center spacing, 4.8 mm bore,
9.5 mm countersink diameter at 90 degrees. Screw holes sit above the receiver.
The keyed socket and latch features are inherited unchanged ahead of the
backing overlap. The source bore is nominally 33.6 mm diameter. These are
source-mesh measurements, not manufacturer dimensions.

PRINT PROFILE
H2D, black PLA, LEFT nozzle; 0.20 mm layers, 5 walls, 25% gyroid, 5 top/bottom
layers. Upright socket, 3 mm outer brim, supports disabled. A roughly 2.1 mm
inherited latch lip is the main small overhang to inspect on the first print.
Actual filament mapping must be checked before sending a job.

STATUS
One watertight connected body per holder; source socket preservation checked.
Both projects slice without plate warnings or support toolpaths, using only
Bambu PLA Basic / LEFT extruder 1. Estimated single: 1 h 39 min 27 sec / 43.39 g;
pair: 3 h 13 min 57 sec / 86.76 g. Records are under Validation/single and
Validation/pair. These are slicer estimates, not actual print results.
No print has been sent. User's accessory fit and installed use are untested.
IP1251-to-IP3251 compatibility is inferred from identical Shark part numbers:
Duster Crevice Tool 576CH1000 and Pet Multi-Tool 579CH1000.
