SHARK POWERDETECT ACCESSORY MOUNT — REFERENCE FILES
Downloaded and inspected: 2026-11-18

1. Shark Powerdetect Vacuum (IP1251) Attachment Holder
Creator: jverlenich
Original: https://www.printables.com/model/1421739-shark-powerdetect-vacuum-ip1251-attachment-holder
Files: vacuumattach_l.3mf; vacuumattach_r.3mf
License: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

The original model attaches accessory receivers to the vacuum's wand with a
split clamp, two M3 x 16 mm screws and nuts (or small zip ties). The author
designed it for an IP1251, printed it in PLA, and did not claim compatibility
with every PowerDetect variant. An independent five-star make by Konrad Stang
reports successful printing and attachment fit; the review does not identify
the reviewer's exact vacuum variant:
https://www.printables.com/make/3354408

These 3MF files declare METERS, not millimeters. Multiply their coordinates by
1000 when importing into a millimeter CAD workflow. The receiver is a female
socket around the accessory's exterior connector, not a male insertion nub.
Measured from vacuumattach_r.3mf, the keyed circular bore is approximately
33.6 mm diameter, 15 mm long, with two 3.35 mm wide keyways and 39.2 mm total
span across the keyways. These are dimensions of the published printed model,
not manufacturer specifications or measurements of the user's accessories.

Any derived cabinet-mount geometry must retain attribution to jverlenich,
identify modifications, and comply with the noncommercial/share-alike license.
The original source files in this directory have not been modified.

2. SHARK PowerDetect Vacuum Accessories Wall Mount
Creator: Keremit
Original: https://www.printables.com/model/1496565-shark-powerdetect-vacuum-accessories-wall-mount
Files: SHARK IP1251EUT PowerDetect_Halterung.3mf;
       powerdetect_fusion_files.f3d
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The author identifies the vacuum as IP1251EUT, reports printing the two-piece
dovetail assembly in PETG and describes it as sturdy. No independent makes or
comments were present when inspected. Its 350 mm shelf has three keyed female
openings: approximately 34.6 mm circular diameter, 3.35 mm keyway width and
40.1 mm total span across the keyways. The 3MF declares millimeters. This model
is a dimensional comparison/reference, not the source of the compact receiver.
The original source files in this directory have not been modified.

IP3251 COMPATIBILITY — INFERENCE, NOT A VERIFIED FIT
Shark lists Duster Crevice Tool 576CH1000 and Pet Multi-Tool 579CH1000 for both
IP1251 and IP3251. Shared accessory part numbers support trying an IP1251
receiver with the user's IP3251 accessories, but the user's exact physical
accessories have not yet been test-fitted. These published receiver models do
not establish the dimensions of the accessories' inner opening, so they do
not provide a verified male-nub design.
https://www.sharkninja.com/duster-crevice-tool/576CH1000.html
https://www.sharkninja.com/pet-multi-tool/579CH1000.html

The author's published model dimensions and print reports should not be
represented as Shark engineering drawings or a guarantee of fit.
