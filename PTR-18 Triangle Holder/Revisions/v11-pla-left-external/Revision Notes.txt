PTR-18 PLUS — v11 PLA BASIC / LEFT EXTERNAL FEED
Created October 15, 2026; feedback updated October 16, 2026

Project: PTR18_Hanger_v11_PLA_Basic_Left_TEST_FIT.3mf
The user explicitly requested starting the full PLA print while away from
the computer. Bambu Studio's device UI showed the available external spool
as black Bambu PLA Basic feeding the LEFT nozzle. This project maps the
active PLA filament slot 1 to left extruder 1, matching that feed. The v10
right-nozzle project is preserved separately.
Both project and plate use Manual filament grouping, so offline slicing cannot
automatically move the PLA back to the default right nozzle.

UNCHANGED FROM v10
Geometry, object placement, process settings and existing filament calibration
are identical. The STL is copied without changes. Print_Preview.png is the
same actual-CAD view; its blue display color is for visibility.
The four pin pockets are 11.8 mm diameter with two 8 mm and two 16 mm depths.
The six small screw pockets are 8.8 mm diameter and 6 mm deep. The measured
4 mm screw heads sit 2 mm below the shelf for retrieval by their threads.
Cleats, cutout hook, fence channel and Allen-key hole remain unchanged.

PLA SETUP
Bambu PLA Basic @BBL H2D, black, left 0.4 mm standard nozzle, external feed.
Saved temperatures: 220 C nozzle and 55 C Textured PEI bed.
Saved process: 0.20 mm layers, 6 walls, 30% gyroid, 6 top/bottom layers,
100 mm/s outer-wall limit, automatic tree supports and 6 mm outer brim.
Original PLA flow ratio 1.029 and pressure advance 0.04 remain in the file.
No new filament calibration is claimed by this revision.

CHECKS AND STATUS
Source/prepare_pla_v11.py reproducibly copies v10 and verifies unchanged
geometry, placement, process and filament calibration. Its --slice option
uses a separate temporary CLI profile and validates that only PLA filament 1
and left extruder 1 are used. Validation/settings.json and slicing.json hold
the completed slice results; slice_info.config records the nozzle and material.
The successful slice has no plate warnings and uses only PLA filament 1 on
left extruder 1: 10 h 53 min 23 sec / 420.77 g.
Generated G-code stays outside the library. Final project/STL/preview hashes
are in Validation/archive_hashes.json.

The one-notch diameter sample was accepted in PETG. The new 6 mm screw depth,
thread retrieval, full PLA holder fit and loaded behavior still require
physical evaluation. Completion and positive appearance are recorded below.
Preparation and slicing do not establish a load rating.
