STORAGE
- Two 16 mm hanging shafts with 19.2 mm retaining heads, 192 mm apart.
  Based on TSO's published 20 mm holes and 96 mm spacing. There is 24 mm
  clear space between each root face and retaining shoulder.
- Open-ended fence cradle: 40 mm clear front-to-back, 17 mm retaining lips.
  A longer fence may overhang the ends. This is a chosen storage capacity;
  TSO does not publish the detached fence's cross-section or length.
- Eight 6.6 mm clearance holes for M6 pins, six 4.6 mm holes for M4 screws,
  two 8.8 mm holes for possible M8 extras, and a 3.8 mm hex-key hole.
  Hardware drops into the holes; the printed holes are not threaded.
- Small loose-parts tray with approximately 92 x 13 mm clear floor area.
  Extra attachment types and head diameters are unknown, so simultaneous
  capacity depends on actual size. M8 positions are spare capacity, not a
  claim that the standard PTR kit contains M8 hardware.

FRENCH CLEAT
Designed around the user's nominal 3/4-inch plywood wall rail and assumed
45-degree bevel. Two integrated 70 mm-wide hooks project 17.5 mm behind the back.
The back bears against the FRONT of the wooden rail below its bevel; it does
not use full-depth wall spacers. Actual wood thickness, bevel, rail height and
clearance to fasteners must be checked with the cleat sample.

HOW TO CHECK THE SAMPLES
1. Cleat sample: confirm it settles on the rail with bevel contact and its flat
   back supported by the front of the rail. It should not rock or bottom out
   against the wall before the bevel seats.
2. Peg sample: pass its head through a large triangle hole. The triangle should
   settle around the smaller shaft behind the head and lift off intentionally.
   Hold the tool securely for this test; the sample is not a mounted hanger.
3. Fence channel: check the detached fence sits securely in the 40 mm channel
   and can be removed without catching. This sample is a short cross-section.
4. Hardware sample: test pins, screws, key and extras. Its four holes are 4.6,
   6.6, 8.8 and 3.8 mm; the largest hole makes the sequence easy to identify.
   Its 6 mm thickness and hole print orientation match the main shelf.

FULL-ASSEMBLY FIT CHECK
The single-peg sample cannot verify pair spacing or clearance below the shelf.
Intended tool position is a straight leg across the top, triangle point down,
using the first and third large holes along that leg from the right-angle corner
(one hole skipped). Check the actual tool against the 192 mm peg spacing.
Near the backing, braces permit about 66.8 mm from peg-hole centre to the top
edge while allowing 2 mm to lift the tool. Actual hole-to-edge offsets are not
published. Check the triangle hangs freely and lifts off without striking the
shelf or braces before relying on the full hanger.

PRINT SETTINGS
Bambu Lab H2D, 0.4 mm nozzle; Bambu PLA Basic in filament slot 1.
0.20 mm layers, 6 walls, 30% gyroid, 6 top and 6 bottom layers.
Automatic tree supports, critical regions only, 30-degree threshold; supports
may start on the model. 6 mm outer brim with 0.1 mm gap.
Side orientation keeps the pegs' gravity-bending plane within the layers.
It does not establish a load rating. Remove support remnants from the peg
heads, shelf and cleat contact surfaces before checking fit.
Estimates depend on actual printer and filament. No print was sent to a printer.

VALIDATION
Main hanger and each sample are single watertight, consistently oriented solids.
Main hardware holes and sample holes were independently checked for clear
through-passages. Both projects slice with return code 0 and no slice warnings
in Bambu Studio 02.08.02.61. Reports are under Revisions/v01-test-fit/Validation.
These checks establish digital printability, not physical fit or strength.
