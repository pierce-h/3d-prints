PTR-18 ATTACHMENT CLEARANCE TEST — v08
October 15, 2026

Project: PTR18_Attachment_Clearance_Test_v08_PETG_Right.3mf
This is a pair of small fit samples. The full holder remains v07.

WHY THIS TEST
The user finished the v07 PETG Matte print. The shape is right, it feels strong,
and the surface is better than expected, with visible lines still present.
All attachment pockets are reported about 2–3 mm too narrow. This is an
estimate, not a measured opening or attachment diameter. Loaded strength has
not been established by this feedback.

The actual v07 file has four 9.8 mm pin pockets and six 6.8 mm screw pockets,
with no scale error. These were based on the earlier 9 mm and 6 mm body
measurements and provide 0.8 mm nominal diametral clearance. Holes print
horizontally in the saved side pose; sagging roofs or support remnants are
possible contributors, but the cause of the physical shortfall is unconfirmed.

TWO SAMPLES
A — raised label 2; ONE corner notch at the small-screw end:
  Short pin: 11.8 mm diameter x 8 mm deep
  Tall pin:  11.8 mm diameter x 16 mm deep
  Screw:      8.8 mm diameter x 2.5 mm deep
  All diameters are 2 mm larger than v07.

B — raised label 3; TWO corner notches at the small-screw end:
  Short pin: 12.8 mm diameter x 8 mm deep
  Tall pin:  12.8 mm diameter x 16 mm deep
  Screw:      9.8 mm diameter x 2.5 mm deep
  All diameters are 3 mm larger than v07.

Each shelf bank is 72 x 24 mm, with the original 6 mm shelf thickness and
3 mm deep-pocket walls/floors. The shallow screw floor is 3.5 mm. An end foot
provides flat bed contact. The base of each bank is 19 x 24 x 72 mm tall;
the small raised label projects about 0.6 mm from its face.
The pocket axes are horizontal during printing, as on the full holder.

PRINT AND TEST
Saved setup: Bambu H2D / 0.4 mm, black Bambu PETG Matte (project filament 4),
RIGHT nozzle. Original 0.20 mm layers, 6 walls, 30% gyroid, tree supports and
6 mm brim retained. Outer-wall speed is limited to 100 mm/s, down from the
v07 standard-nozzle limit of 200 mm/s; actual speed may be lower.

Current LABELED slice: 1 h 41 min 51 sec / 32.94 g for both samples, including
preparation and supports. Success, no plate warnings, filament 4/right extruder 2.

PRE-LABEL Bambu slice estimate for BOTH samples: 1 h 42 min 12 sec / 32.89 g,
including preparation and supports. That slice succeeded with no plate warning
and used only PETG filament 4 and right extruder 2. This estimate predates the
labels; use the separate labeled slicing records for the saved labeled project.
A print was observed underway that began before the labels were added. No
completed-print or fit result has been reported. Confirm the actual PETG spool
mapping when sending a future print.

Let the parts cool, remove supports from the openings, and turn the samples
so their pockets face up. Insert attachment bodies first, with threads up.
Try the short pins, tall pins and screw heads separately in both banks.
Choose the smallest opening for each type that allows easy insertion and
removal. Report A or B for each type; neither or both too loose is also useful.
Depths are unchanged. The fit result will determine the full-holder revision.

The smaller samples cannot reproduce every surface effect of the 260 mm-tall
holder, so their finish alone will not establish the cause of the earlier bands.

FINISHING THE EXISTING PRINT
Light wet-sanding is a practical way to reduce ridges on broad faces. Start
on a hidden patch to assess the effect on matte-black appearance. Preserve
cleat mating surfaces and pocket depths. A change to 0.16 mm layers may make
ordinary layer texture finer but is not a confirmed fix for the irregular bands.
PETG sanding reference: https://help.prusa3d.com/article/petg_2059
