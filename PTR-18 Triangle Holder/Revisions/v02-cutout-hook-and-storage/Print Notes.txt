TRIANGLE SUPPORT
An 80 mm-wide shelf hooks into the triangle's cutout. It has an 8 mm clear
plate gap, an 8 mm-thick bearing floor and a 10 mm retaining lip.
The user described the plate as roughly the same thickness as the other TSO
tool. Its exact thickness and usable cutout dimensions remain unknown; 8 mm
is a design clearance, not a measured plate thickness.

The full model allows up to 77 mm from the cutout's upper bearing edge to the
triangle's outer top edge, while allowing 12 mm upward movement for removal.
This assumes the actual cutout admits the hook and the triangle can lift clear
of its 10 mm lip. The hook sample checks local fit across the full 80 mm width;
it does not reproduce the storage shelf overhead. Check that clearance on the
full assembly before relying on it.

STORAGE
- Detached fence: user measured 430 x 20 x 10 mm. It stores on its 10 mm edge
  in a 12 mm-wide open-ended channel, with 13 mm retaining lips. Its 20 mm
  height leaves 7 mm exposed above the lips. Center it so it overhangs the
  260 mm holder by 85 mm at each end.
- Eight pin-body pockets, 12 mm diameter: four 6 mm deep nearer the fence,
  four 13 mm deep in the front row. Pins drop in body first, threads up.
- Six screw-head pockets, 8.5 mm diameter x 4 mm deep, for screws stored
  head down with threads up.
- Two extra-attachment pockets, 16 mm diameter x 13 mm deep.
- Allen-key pocket: closed bottom, 35 mm deep, with a 40 x 8 mm opening
  envelope. The 45-degree pointed ends leave 32 mm of full-width opening.
  Keys drop in long end first; check that their bends stay accessible.
- A shallow loose-parts tray with approximately 92 x 13 mm clear floor area.

Attachment-body, screw-head, extra and Allen-key dimensions are not confirmed.
These are pocket capacities, not a guarantee that every attachment fits.
The closed bottoms support the bodies/heads; none of the pockets is threaded.

FRENCH CLEAT
Designed around the user's nominal 3/4-inch plywood wall rail and assumed
45-degree bevel. Two integrated 70 mm-wide hooks project 17.5 mm behind the back.
The back bears against the FRONT of the wooden rail below its bevel. Actual
wood thickness, bevel, rail height and fastener clearance need the cleat test.

HOW TO CHECK THE SAMPLES
1. Cleat_Fit: confirm the bevel seats and the flat back rests against the rail.
   It should not rock or hit the wall before its bevel seats.
2. Cutout_Hook_Fit: hold the triangle securely and test the 80 mm-wide hook in
   the intended cutout. The plate must settle into the 8 mm gap and lift over
   the retaining lip without binding. The sample is not a mounted hanger.
3. Fence_Channel_Fit: place the fence upright, 20 mm tall and 10 mm across the
   12 mm channel. Check easy removal. This is a short cross-section sample.
4. Attachment_Pockets_Fit: test the four pocket types with bodies/heads down,
   threads up. The pockets are 12 x 6, 12 x 13, 8.5 x 4 and 16 x 13 mm
   (diameter x depth). Confirm short parts stay easy to grasp.
5. Allen_Key_Pocket_Fit: test the actual keys together and separately. Confirm
   they drop freely, remain accessible and can be removed without snagging.

PRINT SETTINGS
Bambu Lab H2D, 0.4 mm nozzle; Bambu PLA Basic in filament slot 1.
0.20 mm layers, 6 walls, 30% gyroid, 6 top and 6 bottom layers.
Automatic tree supports, critical regions only, 30-degree threshold; supports
may start on the model. 6 mm outer brim with 0.1 mm gap.
Keep the saved side orientation. Remove support remnants from the cutout hook,
fence channel and cleat mating surfaces before checking fit. Inspect pocket
openings for strings or rough edges. Estimates depend on printer and filament.

VALIDATION
The main hanger and five samples are single watertight solids. Independent
checks confirmed the hook, channel, pocket clearances and closed floors.
Both projects slice with return code 0 and no slice warnings. A toolpath check
found no support-extrusion endpoints inside the storage pockets. Validation
reports are under Revisions/v02-cutout-hook-and-storage/Validation.
These checks establish digital printability, not physical fit or a load rating.
