PTR-18 PLUS — v03 MEASURED ATTACHMENTS
October 14, 2026

Changes from v02
User measurements exclude the threads:
- Two 9 mm diameter x 25 mm tall bodies: 9.8 x 16 mm pockets, 9 mm exposed.
- Two 9 mm diameter x 12 mm tall bodies: 9.8 x 8 mm pockets, 4 mm exposed.
- Six 6 mm diameter x 4 mm tall heads: 6.8 x 2.5 mm pockets, 1.5 mm exposed.
Pocket dimensions are diameter x depth. The 0.8 mm diameter clearance is
0.4 mm per side. All parts drop in body/head first with threads pointing up.

The cutout hook, measured-fence channel, Allen-key slot, tray, four 12 mm spare
pockets and two 16 mm spare pockets are unchanged from v02.

The quick-check piece tests the three measured pocket types only. It is also
included on the full fit-check plate. Keep the saved side orientation and
0.20 mm / 6 wall / 30% gyroid settings for comparable fit.

All three projects slice successfully without warnings. Physical printed fit
and loaded strength are unverified. The 8 mm hook gap and 77 mm top-bar
capacity with 12 mm removal lift remain design assumptions. The hook sample
does not reproduce the overhead shelf. Extra attachments and Allen keys still
need fit checks. See ../../Print Notes.txt for the complete procedure.
