PTR-18 PLUS — v09 ENLARGED AND DEEPER POCKETS
October 15, 2026

Project: PTR18_Hanger_v09_Enlarged_Deeper_Pockets_PETG_Right_TEST_FIT.3mf
Status: GEOMETRY VERIFIED AND SLICE-TESTED. Full revision and new screw depth not yet printed.

WHY THIS REVISION
The first full v07 print had attachment pockets the user estimated 2–3 mm too
narrow. In the subsequent v08 clearance test, the ONE-NOTCH / +2 mm sample had
more than enough space and was a touch loose, which the user accepted.
The user also requested two to three times the shallow screw-pocket depth and
confirmed: "Recess them; grab the threads."

STORAGE CHANGES
- Two short-pin pockets: 11.8 mm diameter x 8 mm deep.
- Two tall-pin pockets: 11.8 mm diameter x 16 mm deep.
- Six screw-head pockets: 8.8 mm diameter x 6 mm deep.
All diameters are 2 mm larger than v07. Pin depths are unchanged. Screw depth
increases from 2.5 to 6 mm, or 2.4 times the former depth. The measured 4 mm screw
heads sit 2 mm below the shelf; exposed thread height equals the actual thread
length minus that recess. Thread lengths have not been measured, and retrieval
at the new depth still needs a physical check.

Wider pin bosses preserve 3 mm floors and nominal 3 mm radial walls. New screw
bosses beneath the 6 mm shelf preserve 3.5 mm floors and nominal 3 mm radial
walls. Cavities are cut after unioning the added material so the nearby gusset
does not block the left rear screw pocket. That gusset remains connected.
The 72-sided cylinders have approximately 2.997 mm minimum facet-to-facet wall
thickness; dimensions above state their nominal radial thickness.

There are still exactly ten attachment pockets and one 4.2 mm Allen-key through-
hole. Pocket positions, cutout hook, French cleats, fence channel and overall
bounds are unchanged. Mounted size is 260 x 135 x 99.5 mm. Saved print pose is
one side down, with a 135 x 99.5 mm footprint and 260 mm height. Pocket axes are
horizontal while printing. Existing v04 cleat, hook, fence and key samples remain
applicable; their saved material settings and estimates are PLA. The old v04
attachment sample retains the superseded smaller diameters.

PRINT SETUP
Settings and filament extras are copied unchanged from the saved native v08
clearance-test project: black Bambu PETG Matte, project filament 4, RIGHT nozzle.
Bambu H2D / 0.4 mm, 0.20 mm layers, 6 walls, 30% gyroid, 6 top/bottom layers,
automatic tree supports at critical regions, and 6 mm outer brim with 0.1 mm gap.
Outer-wall speed remains limited to 100 mm/s. No extra temperature, flow or
cooling changes are introduced. Confirm actual spool mapping before printing.
Slice result and estimate: success, no plate warnings; 10 h 44 min 40 sec / 422.39 g,
using only filament 4 / right extruder 2.

FINISH FEEDBACK
The v08 sample is smoother than the earlier full print, but rough lines and
some waviness remain near the holes. The cause is unconfirmed. The horizontal
pockets make overhangs, support contact and local cooling/speed changes plausible
contributors; moisture remains possible. Smaller samples do not reproduce all
effects of the full holder, so improvement is not proof of one setting's effect.
Reference for PETG overhangs, supports and sanding:
https://help.prusa3d.com/article/petg_2059
