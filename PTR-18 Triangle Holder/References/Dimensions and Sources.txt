PUBLISHED / CONFIRMED
- Nominal 18-inch (457.2 mm) triangle leg.
- Eight 20 mm holes on 96 mm centres; used for v01 pegs, not the v02 cutout hook.
- Standard kit: two short and two long M6 locating pins; long pins are 25 mm.
- Six M4 x 0.7 socket-head fence screws and a 3 mm hex key.
- Removable foot/fence, part 610-184B.

  two 9 mm diameter x 25 mm tall; two 9 mm diameter x 12 mm tall;

- Attachment pockets should have closed bottoms, with threads pointing up.

  This supersedes the earlier closed-bottom slot request.
- Remove the short rectangular loose-parts tray beside the two large pockets.
  Retain the flat shelf and detached-fence channel.

v06 DESIGN CAPACITIES
- Hook: 80 mm width, 8 mm plate gap, 10 mm retaining lip.
- Up to 77 mm outer-top-edge to cutout-bearing-edge distance with an assumed
  12 mm upward removal movement; actual cutout fit remains unconfirmed.
- Fence channel: 12 mm clear, 13 mm lips, 260 mm long and open-ended.
- Two 9.8 mm tall-pin pockets, 16 mm deep; 9 mm of the measured body exposed.
- Two 9.8 mm short-pin pockets, 8 mm deep; 4 mm of the measured body exposed.
- Six 6.8 mm screw-head pockets, 2.5 mm deep; 1.5 mm of the measured head exposed.
- Dedicated measured pockets have 0.8 mm total diameter clearance.
- Layout in installed coordinates: pin columns X=-98/-66, short row Z=40,
  tall row Z=64; screw-head columns X=22/58/94, rows Z=40/64. Units millimetres.
  Ten attachment pockets plus one key hole; no spare pockets or loose-parts tray.
- Allen key: straight 4.2 mm through-hole through the 6 mm shelf, intended for
  the standard 3 mm key. Long leg passes below; bend rests above. No bottom,
  enclosing cup or chamfer. Attachment pockets retain their closed bottoms.
Confirm these capacities against the actual tool and accessories using the
fit samples. They do not establish physical fit or loaded strength.
